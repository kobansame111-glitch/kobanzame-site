Looking for a vintage shop in Kawagoe?

Kobanzame is a vintage clothing shop in Motomachi, Kawagoe, Saitama, set in an 80-year-old wooden house. We carry vintage clothing sourced in Japan and abroad, and GAW, our own line of remade accessories. It is an easy stop while you explore Kawagoe.

Location and hours

1-14-5 Motomachi, Kawagoe, Saitama 350-0062, Japan, in the Motomachi area of Kawagoe, the same area as Kawagoe’s old kurazukuri (clay-walled merchant house) streets. Open 12:00–20:00, closed every Monday and Tuesday (open every day from 1 November 2026). Changes are announced on Instagram (@remora__used372).

What you will find

Mostly vintage clothing from the US and Europe, for men and women: outerwear, shirts and tees, dresses, shoes and small goods. We choose by material, texture and the feel of each piece rather than brand or trend, so everything is one of a kind.

Prices

Pieces listed on this site range from ¥2,200 to ¥69,080 (tax and domestic shipping included). In-store prices vary by item.

New arrivals

New pieces arrive every Wednesday, and go up on this site on Wednesday morning.

GAW

Our own accessory line: old materials such as spoons remade into pendants, bangles and more. Each one is one of a kind.

Holding a piece

Every piece is one of a kind, so we do not hold items, online or in the shop. First come, first served.

Buying online

Pieces on this site can be bought by credit card through Square. Online checkout ships within Japan; for overseas shipping, please contact us before buying.
