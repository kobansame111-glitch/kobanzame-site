Workshops

Make something in our old wooden shop. You take your piece home the same day.

Brass bangle workshop

¥1,500tax incl., for the 24 Oct sessions
About 60 min · up to 2 people per session

The owner bends a cut brass bar to the shape of your wrist in front of you, and you stamp the finishing marks (letters, numbers, symbols) yourself. Take it home the same day.

  • One bangle per person, stamping included
  • We may take photos of your hands while you work (tell us if you prefer not)
  • If you have a metal allergy, please ask us before booking
  • Brass changes colour as you wear it

Natural stone bead bracelet workshop

¥1,200tax incl., for the 24 Oct sessions
About 30 min · up to 2 people per session

Choose one kind of natural stone bead from what we have in the shop that day and make your own bracelet. Take it home the same day.

  • The price includes one kind of stone
  • Each additional kind is +100 yen, paid in the shop on the day
  • The stones on offer depend on what is in stock that day
