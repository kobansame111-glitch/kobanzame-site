The story

Gohan, drawn in blue, stares out from the chest. A ©2001 FUNimation-licensed Dragon Ball Z vintage tee.

Its past life

Made by Olaes Design, which produced many anime tees in the US in the early 2000s. Putting a blue Gohan centre stage is an unusual layout, and the back is packed with Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo and Majin Buu. More than twenty years later, it has washed up at our shop in Kawagoe.

Details

The colourful print stands out on the yellow body. 100% cotton, easy to wear on its own. Print and fabric are both in good condition.

Who it's for

The Dragon Ball generation, and anyone into anime tees or Y2K. Unisex.
