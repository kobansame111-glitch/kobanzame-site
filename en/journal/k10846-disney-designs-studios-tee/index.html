The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. On the chest, Mickey holds a clapperboard like a film director. A 1990s Disney DESIGNS shirt from The Walt Disney Studios line.

What it is

Disney DESIGNS was a label that made official Disney merchandise in the 1990s. This one carries a film-making motif from The Walt Disney Studios. The navy base is calm, and the original print is still clearly there. Made in the USA.

Details

Solid 100% cotton fabric. The quiet navy makes a character tee easy to wear as an adult. There is a small pinhole near the seam at the top of the left shoulder.

Who it's for

Anyone who loves Disney, film or 90s vintage, and wants a character tee in a calm colour. Unisex.
