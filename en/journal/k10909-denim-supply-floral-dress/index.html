The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. Denim & Supply was a Ralph Lauren line that ran only from 2011 to 2016. This is a classic dress from it, with small flowers on black.

Details

Button front, gathered neckline and softly full cuffs. Drapey 100% viscose gives it shape on its own. A seam at the waist keeps it neat, and it runs long at 111 cm.

How to wear

On its own in spring and autumn, open as a light layer in summer, with a turtleneck and boots in winter.

Size and condition

Tagged S/P, a standard small. The hem is 120 cm wide, so it swings as you walk. Rank A, no notable damage.
