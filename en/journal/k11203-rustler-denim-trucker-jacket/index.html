The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. A RUSTLER from the 1970s–80s, with the indigo still deep — a denim jacket you can wear in to your own colour.

The brand

RUSTLER was Wrangler's sister brand, making honest, affordable workwear. This jacket is from the 1970s–80s, and much of its deep indigo remains.

Details

Sturdy 13 oz denim. Two flap chest pockets, hand pockets and RUSTLER-stamped buttons — plain, classic construction. A few small marks and light wear around the collar and shoulders; overall fading is minimal and the fabric is solid.

Who it's for

For people who want to wear in a dark denim jacket their own way. Throw it over a plain T-shirt; it works with denim or work trousers between seasons.
