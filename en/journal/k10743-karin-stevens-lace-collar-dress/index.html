The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. A classic all-over print of small flowers on navy, and a white lace collar that steals the show. An 1980s floral dress by Karin Stevens.

Details

The large Battenberg-style lace collar is sewn on, designed for this dress. Puff sleeves and a smocked waist give it a soft 1980s silhouette.

How it feels

Drapey 100% rayon, so the hem moves as you walk. The elastic smocked waist is comfortable. Tagged US10, fitting a Japanese M–L, and the stretchy waist suits a range of body types. Dry clean only.

Who it's for

Wear it on its own for a classic look, or break it up with sneakers. Rank A, no notable damage.
