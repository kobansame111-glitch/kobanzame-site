The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. You can tell it at a glance by the C patch on the sleeve: a 1990s Champion short-sleeve sweatshirt, made in USA.

Its past life

The neck tag carries the era's line "IT TAKES A LITTLE MORE TO MAKE A CHAMPION U.S.A." A small C logo on the chest and the C patch on the sleeve — details for people who prefer the real thing over flash.

Details

A relaxed raglan shape, pulled in by ribbed cuffs and hem. Grey fabric of a comfortable weight. The ribbed hem means it wears shorter than the length suggests. Some fading and softening from age, and a discreet repair inside one cuff. No major damage.

Who it's for

Wear it with denim or wide trousers, or layer a shirt underneath. Unisex.
