The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. Natural-tone python on the vamp and collar: vintage western boots from Adams Boots, made in Mexico.

Design

Python skin paired with brown leather, with wingtip-style overlays and feather-like stitching. A long pointed toe. The shading of the scales sits next to the shine of well-worn leather.

Condition

Scuffs at the heels and toes, creasing and general wear. No noticeable chips or major damage to the python.

How to wear them

Roll up your denim to show the python, or use them to loosen up slacks or a dress. For anyone.
