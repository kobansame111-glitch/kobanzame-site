The story

A sterling silver bracelet we sourced in Turkey. A local artisan finished it by hand: a one-of-a-kind piece that looks like lace.

The handwork

Each petal and leaf is shaped from fine wire, set in place and soldered one by one. It is a craft with fewer makers every year, and rarely seen in Japan.

On the wrist

It looks delicate, but it has a quiet presence once you put it on. For women and men alike.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a little brightness at the wrist, and a piece that still shows the maker's hand.
