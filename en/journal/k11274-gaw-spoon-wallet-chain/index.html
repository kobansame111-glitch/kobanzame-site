The story

After the pendant, another spoon has been reborn. This time it hangs at the hip instead of the chest.

Its past life

A single spoon from someone's dinner table. The engraving on the handle is still there; we kept it when reshaping the spoon into the hook.

Its new life

Paired with a brass curb chain and snap hook. The spoon's silver tone next to the antique gold of the brass gives it a depth a new chain doesn't have. Handmade by Kobanzame for the GAW line.

Fit

About 49 cm long: it reaches a wallet in the front pocket from the belt loop and hangs with a natural drape. Works with denim, slacks or wide trousers.

The next chapter is yours

The chain is new; the hook is the original spoon, so it shows fine scratches and dullness from age. Brass darkens beautifully with use.
