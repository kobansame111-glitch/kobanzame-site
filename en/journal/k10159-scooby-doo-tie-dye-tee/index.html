The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. Tie-dye, peace signs and a grinning Scooby. A Scooby-Doo tee from 1997.

Its past life

Made by Changes, a company behind many American character tees in the 90s, under official Hanna-Barbera licence. Blue and purple tie-dye scattered with peace signs and SD logos, in a hippie mood. Scooby smiles on the front and looks back over his tail on the back. The tag is dated 1997.

Details

A slightly oversized cut that slips straight into vintage styling today. The print shows cracking from age, but there is no major damage to the fabric or seams.

Fans of tie-dye, character tees and 90s vintage looking for something no one else has. Unisex.
