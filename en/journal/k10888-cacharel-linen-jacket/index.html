Some vintage clothes have never been worn. This one still has its basting stitches in the pockets and hem.

Its past life

Cacharel is a French brand known for florals and light, easy clothes. This is a jacket from the 1990s that has been waiting, basting still in, to be worn for the first time.

The shell is 100% linen. Mustard looks difficult but turns any outfit into a picture over jeans and a white T-shirt. The creases of linen are part of its character. Lining: viscose and acetate.
