The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. Vintage boots from Tony Lama, one of America's classic western bootmakers: glossy black leather with cream stitching across the shaft.

Details

Cream-thread stitchwork covers the shaft. The texture of worn-in leather and the shading at the toes give it a true vintage face. Made in USA, style number 2916.

Condition

Scuffs at the toes and heels, creasing and general wear. No noticeable tears or major sole damage.

How to wear them

Let a little of the shaft stitching show under your denim hem. They also loosen up slacks or a dress. For anyone.
