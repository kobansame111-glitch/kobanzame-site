The story

We are Kobanzame, a vintage shop in an old wooden house in Kawagoe. A medium indigo with natural fading from the thighs to the knees. Made-in-USA Levi's 505.

The 505

The 505 is known as the zip-fly counterpart to the button-fly 501, and has long been a staple easy straight. This pair was made in the USA and handled by Levi Strauss Japan, with leather patch and red tab. A regular straight leg that falls cleanly to the hem.

Size and condition

Tagged W30 L36. Measured flat: waist 36 cm, rise 33 cm, thigh 25 cm, total length 78 cm (waist and thigh are one side; double for the full circumference). Rank A. Clean vintage with natural fading and wear marks.

Who it's for

An honest straight leg: true to size for men, relaxed for women. Easy with a T-shirt, all year round.
